# AUD-002 — Permanent Mercenary passive-sheet input contract

## Status

blocked

Contract version: `1.0.0`.

The current public record establishes that a Luminary can maintain a permanent
Mercenary and that the Mercenary has an information sheet. It does not identify
the relevant sheet labels, units, display precision, equipment inclusion, or
condition/update rules. Those unanswered load-bearing claims block a derived
Mercenary-sheet calculation, while leaving a carefully labelled manual final
input path available to a later BUILD.

## Question

For one active permanent Mercenary, which passive-sheet fields can safely
provide Fire Resistance, Uncapped Fire Resistance, Maximum Fire Resistance,
equipment inclusion, and condition context to the initial Enmity's Embrace
path?

## Product impact

This audit bounds the future Mercenary manual-input form, mapped-equipment
adapter, Enmity calculation input adapter, and missing-information/review-state
UI. It does not model general Mercenary DPS or infer ownership from a PoB item
set.

## Scope

Included are the existence and high-level purpose of the permanent-Mercenary
information sheet; the exact first-release inputs that remain unverified; a
non-double-counting manual workaround; and a controlled-observation request.

Excluded are a player-sheet analogy, parsing a sheet, numeric baseline values,
equipment aggregation, temporary-effect mechanics, passive allocations, UI
implementation, and all calculations.

## Source plan

The audit checked, in order:

1. GGG's 3.29.1 patch notes to freeze the target version;
2. GGG's 3.29.0 content-update notes for the Luminary and permanent-Mercenary
   statements;
3. public reports for a reproducible display observation; and
4. whether any of those sources names the required field labels, units,
   equipment inclusion, or update conditions.

No versioned official UI-string or extracted sheet-field source was located in
the inspected material. Path of Building was deliberately not used as authority
for permanent-Mercenary sheet semantics.

## Evidence

- GGG's 3.29.1 patch notes are the current official release notes at the
  version freeze. They establish the target version, not the unchanged status
  of every 3.29.0 mechanic.
- The 3.29.0 content-update notes state that a Luminary can have up to three
  permanent Mercenaries with one active, may change their equipment, and can
  inspect an information sheet that lists passive stats. The announcement does
  not name Fire Resistance, Uncapped Fire Resistance, Maximum Fire Resistance,
  an equipment-inclusion flag, a precision, a cap state, or an update context.
- The cited community thread contains one anonymous report that a Mercenary
  Defence page did not change when the reporter toggled a player aura. It has
  no preserved screenshot, Mercenary identity, equipment list, realm/mode,
  sheet transcription, or controlled before/after state. It is a lead for the
  observation protocol, not evidence that auras are excluded or that the page
  has any particular semantic.

## Conflicts and gaps

The direct GGG evidence is high quality but deliberately high-level. It does
not answer the fields required for a calculation. The sole public observation
found is uncontrolled and cannot resolve the possible aura/display distinction.
No conflict is resolved by assuming that a player character's Defence page or a
Path of Building output applies to a permanent Mercenary.

### Controlled observation request

Obtain a permissioned, controlled observation for Path of Exile 1 `3.29.1` in
one named realm/mode with one named permanent Mercenary and recorded level.
Keep the player's passive state, zone, player auras, Mercenary skills, and all
temporary effects fixed unless the step deliberately changes one of them.

1. Capture the Mercenary information sheet and every relevant tooltip before a
   known Fire Resistance equipment change; transcribe exact labels, displayed
   values, units, and decimals.
2. Capture the same fields after adding/removing one item with a known Fire
   Resistance contribution, preserving the full item text and equipment slots.
3. Capture the same fields with a known Maximum Fire Resistance change where
   possible, then with a player aura/effect changed separately.
4. Record whether the Mercenary is active, whether Enmity's Embrace is equipped
   and usable, the exact conditions, screenshots' original metadata, and the
   UI context (town, hideout, or combat area).

The resulting images/transcriptions prove only the recorded instance unless
they are corroborated by a versioned UI/data source. They must not be committed
without the owner's permission.

## Analysis

The official statement supports a permanent-Mercenary input flow but not a
derivation rule. Treating an unnamed displayed number as final uncapped
resistance, or adding mapped item text to it, could double count equipment or
omit a conditional contribution. The smallest safe workaround is not an
invented sheet field: it is a manual, user-attested pair of final comparable
values plus explicit context. That workaround does not establish any hidden
game calculation and does not make the derived path available.

Missing means no value was supplied. Unknown means a value may be supplied but
its game semantic or inclusion state is not established. Unsupported means the
product does not implement the proposed derivation. None of these states is
zero or an inactive condition.

## Conclusion

`AUD-002` is blocked for a derived permanent-Mercenary sheet contract. It
supports only the bounded manual-input safety contract in `AUD-002-C06`.
Until `AUD-002-C03`, `AUD-002-C04`, and `AUD-002-C05` are supported, a later
BUILD may retain/ask for final manually attested values but must not derive
Uncapped Fire Resistance from a sheet, equipment, or their combination.

## Non-conclusions

This audit does not establish an exact in-game label; units or precision;
whether equipment, passives, auras, debuffs, or temporary effects are included;
whether sheet values are capped; a base Mercenary resistance; or a general rule
from the anonymous community report. It does not authorize Enmity's derived
calculation path.

### Established rules

- Treat manual final values as an explicitly user-attested state, not as a
  discovered universal sheet label.
- The two resistance values used by one calculation must describe the same
  equipped and conditional state.
- Preserve raw imported item text exactly when practical, with its provenance
  and review state. Recognized values may affect that one reviewed build
  instance; malformed, stale, synthetic, conflicting, or unrecognized material
  requires explicit review or confirmation.
- Never silently add a mapped/manual item contribution to an unknown sheet
  value, and never clamp an observed value to reference metadata.

### Manually required or unsupported behavior

Manual values and measurement context are required. Unsupported behavior
includes identifying a sheet label, deriving from a sheet, combining the sheet
with equipment, inferring a condition state, defaulting a maximum resistance,
or treating a PoB item set as Mercenary ownership.
