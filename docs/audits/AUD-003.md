# AUD-003 — Light Radius, direct Link Skill Buff Effect, and Golden Glory source contract

## Status

supported

Contract version: `1.0.0`.

The official 3.29.1 Default passive-tree export supports a bounded catalog of
Light Radius and direct Link Skill Buff Effect records and the exact Golden
Glory wording. It does not establish a complete current item catalog, current
availability, arithmetic, stacking, rounding, or a Flame Link calculation.

## Question

What versioned first-release source records exist for (1) increased/reduced
Light Radius that could be relevant to Golden Glory and (2) direct Link Skill
Buff Effect, and what exactly does Golden Glory establish about their relation?

## Product impact

This audit supplies separate future source recognizers and review catalogs for
player Light Radius and direct Link Skill Buff Effect. It also supplies the
wording-only Golden Glory reference used by AUD-004. It does not produce a
combined total, Flame Link damage, a recommended item list, or a production
mechanics module.

## Scope

Included are the official 3.29.1 **Default** passive tree, its nested mastery
effects, the captured official Trade filter vocabulary, a bounded supporting
PoB cross-check, the official Crucible migration announcement, and the known
Helmet corruption disagreement.

Excluded are Ruthless and alternate trees, historical trees, availability or
acquisition inference, complete item/unique/implicit/enchant coverage, legacy
roll reconstruction, player/target ownership inference, and all arithmetic or
rounding beyond literal source text.

## Source plan

The audit inspected:

1. GGG developer documentation to establish the official PoE1 passive-tree
   export;
2. the pinned `3.29.1` Default-tree export commit and its `data.json` hash;
3. the official Trade stats vocabulary captured at the version freeze;
4. the 3.29.0 official content update and 2023 Crucible migration notice;
5. a pinned PoB 3.29-supporting snapshot only as a parser/catalog cross-check;
   and
6. GGG's published terms for retention risk, without treating them as legal
   advice.

The audit searched all `stats` and nested `masteryEffects[].stats` records in
the Default tree for the relevant wording. It did not execute any downloaded
code or vendor any source payload.

## Evidence

### Versioned Default-tree evidence

GGG's developer documentation names `grindinggear/skilltree-export` as the
official PoE1 Passive Skill Tree export. Commit
`8bd138b32ea2631455cac5935bfab089f826094f` is labelled `3.29.1`; its Default
`data.json` has 3,390 nodes and the recorded UTF-8 SHA-256
`7e9f755e33152129ebf36c2ebdad639c527e4ad70d274b1fefb860f30ca01122`.

The full Default-tree scan found six numeric Light Radius node records, one
deduplicated Energy Shield Mastery effect, Golden Glory, three direct `5%`
Link Skill Buff Effect nodes, Inspiring Bond, Powerful Bond, and the related
Oath of Fealty condition record. The retained source-catalog artifacts list
the exact node/effect IDs and minimal factual fields.

### Official Trade vocabulary

The official `GET /api/trade/data/stats` capture has no patch-version field.
At 2026-08-03T21:49:42Z it was 2,064,498 UTF-8 bytes with SHA-256
`73b655dc76ebdb71192867d8ef12fcd95c06c229551f080c65d3760deccba56e`.
It exposes Light Radius terms across Explicit, Implicit, Fractured, Enchant,
and Scourge domains and six Crucible direct-Link-effect identifiers. It does
not establish an item's slot, natural range, local/global behavior, current
acquisition, or whether a listed term can be offered as an improvement.

### Related official and supporting evidence

The 3.29.0 content-update notes describe the new Helmet corruption as `20–30%`
increased Light Radius. The pinned PoB 3.29-supporting snapshot instead records
`25–30%`. This audit retains that disagreement rather than selecting a natural
range. PoB maps both increased and reduced Light Radius text to Trade stat
`1263695895`, supporting a signed representation only; it is not authority for
target-version availability or Golden Glory arithmetic.

GGG's Crucible announcement says Crucible did not go core and that existing
items with Crucible trees moved to Standard. That permits a `legacy-or-unknown`
review state, not a current-league offer or a broad current-availability claim.

## Conflicts and gaps

The current official tree supports only a Default-tree catalog. The Trade API is
filter vocabulary, not a release-pinned item catalog. The 3.29.0 Helmet
corruption range conflicts with the supporting PoB data. The repository retains
neither source's table as current reference data.

The following remain unknown and must not be inferred from English wording or
from PoB behavior: Golden Glory arithmetic; whether a basis override changes
its value; direct-effect stack group; cross-path addition/multiplication;
rounding; item availability; local/global applicability; and the active state
of Powerful Bond. The exact `Recently` reminder is known, but its runtime state
must be selected or observed rather than guessed.

GGG's official export has no declared repository licence and the Terms page
does not establish an affirmative redistribution right for raw game material.
The committed records therefore retain minimal identifiers, values, source
locators, hashes, and transformation notes only. This is a risk record, not
legal advice.

## Analysis

The evidence warrants three separate contracts: Light Radius source records,
direct Link Skill Buff Effect source records, and Golden Glory wording. It does
not warrant a combined “link effect” total. A source recognizer may identify a
record, preserve any observed value, and state whether its current applicability
is unknown. It may not clamp the instance to a natural range or promote an
unavailable/legacy record into an offer.

For an active Mercenary target and allocated Golden Glory, literal conversion
text is established. The numeric operation is not. For a non-Mercenary or
unknown target, a numeric Golden Glory result is not assumed. The same
claim-level gate applies to direct Link conditions: the record can be shown,
but a conditionally active numeric contribution is withheld while its state is
unknown.

## Conclusion

`AUD-003` is supported for its bounded 3.29.1 Default-tree catalog and for the
literal Golden Glory wording. It is not a complete current equipment catalog or
a formula contract. `AUD-004` may consume the confirmed node/source identities
and wording, but its definitive scaled output remains gated by
`AUD-003-C08` and `AUD-003-C12`.

## Non-conclusions

This audit does not establish all player item sources, natural ranges,
availability, acquisition, local/global behavior, equipment ownership,
mastery-selection multiplicity, Golden Glory arithmetic, direct-effect
stacking, rounding, Flame Link damage, or DPS. It does not make a historic or
observed modifier a current improvement recommendation.

### Established rules

- Keep Light Radius and direct Link Skill Buff Effect data, values, provenance,
  and reporting separate.
- The exact Default-tree record catalog is bounded by `AUD-003-C03` and
  `AUD-003-C04`; it is not an item completeness claim.
- Golden Glory has a literal Mercenary-target relation under `AUD-003-C02`.
- Preserve imported raw item text exactly as instance material with provenance
  and review state. It is not general mechanics evidence, an availability
  record, a natural range, or ownership proof.
- Natural ranges, where later supported, are metadata only. Never clamp an
  observed value or discard unrecognized source text.
- State conditions explicitly; `unknown` is neither `inactive` nor zero.

### Manually required or unsupported behavior

Manual condition selection/review is required for conditional records. The
contract does not derive Golden Glory arithmetic, direct-effect stacking,
cross-path arithmetic, rounding, availability, or a complete item catalog.
