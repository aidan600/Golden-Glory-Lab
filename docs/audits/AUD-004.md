# AUD-004 - Flame Link calculation contract

## Status

supported

Contract version: `1.0.0`.

This audit supports a bounded Flame Link reference contract and symbolic
component reporting. It does not support a definitive scaled integer granted
damage result for an active permanent Mercenary in PoE1 `3.29.1`.

## Question

Which Flame Link facts can a first release retain and present, and which
inputs, dependencies, and game-behavior gaps must gate its damage-grant result?

## Product impact

This contract supplies a future Flame Link reference panel, review state, and
calculation gate. It permits identifying ordinary gem levels, describing the
level-based flat Fire component and the `5%` source-Maximum-Life component,
and showing standard-quality duration. It does not authorize a final scaled
grant, DPS, total Mercenary damage, a combined build score, or an automatic
exceptional-level calculation.

## Scope

Included are the target-version freeze, a pinned PoB `2.66.0`/3.29.0 data
snapshot, official qualitative Flame Link and Mercenary-Link statements, the
official 3.23 quality change, a current unpinned PoEDB cross-check, and a
historical RePoE cross-check.

Excluded are a claim that PoB is live-game authority, a raw level table,
exceptional-level availability, permanent-Mercenary Link state details,
Golden Glory arithmetic, direct-Link-effect stacking, rounding, enemy
resistance, damage-over-time, DPS, and any production calculation.

## Source plan

The audit inspected the target patch notes; the pinned PoB release/commit and
specific gem, skill, stat-description, and interpolation records; relevant
GGG announcements; a current community display; and a historical extraction
only as a regression cross-check. No upstream code was executed and no raw
game-data table, page copy, image, or asset is retained.

## Evidence

PoB release `2.66.0`, commit
`d1e5dc6ab85f5d6939eefbec0f9986c191db0ca9`, is a reproducible 3.29.0
structured source. Its Flame Link gem record identifies
`Metadata/Items/Gems/SkillGemFlameLink`, granted effect `FlameLink`, a normal
maximum level of `20`, and spell/duration/link/fire tags. Its skill record
maps the minimum and maximum Fire grant and the source-Maximum-Life percentage
to the linked target. The stat description identifies the life term as the
link source's Maximum Life, not current or reserved Life.

The artifact records this source as `sourceDataVersion=3.29.0` and
`versionState=supporting-source-version-mismatch`: its target remains 3.29.1,
but the structured source does not prove live continuity.

The same source has normal rows through level `20` and additional rows through
`40`. The latter existence does not prove validity, acquisition, or first
release treatment for exceptional levels. The audit retains the range and
field mappings rather than a copied data table. Its documented rendered
anchors are level `1`: `23-35` added Fire Damage, and level `20`: `169-254`;
they are evidence locators, not a shipped level table. To reproduce those
anchors without retaining the table, inspect `Gems.lua#L13606`,
`act_str.lua#L4283`, `active_skill_gem_stat_descriptions.lua#L5613`, and the
`CalcTools.lua#L183-L205`/`Misc.lua#L29-L30` interpolation support at the
pinned commit. The procedure uses the Flame Link record's interpolation mode
and level requirements; the raw effectiveness values are not player-facing
added-Fire values.

Official 3.16 material qualitatively describes Flame Link's linked-target and
life behavior. The 3.23 quality announcement establishes that standard quality
adds base duration; the pinned record represents this as `75` milliseconds per
quality, or `1,500` milliseconds at quality `20`. Neither source proves the
order or rounding of final scaled added damage. The 3.26 Mercenary FAQ supports
that Link Skills may target an allied Mercenary, but is not a complete 3.29.1
permanent-Link behavior proof.

PoB's release data and the current community display agree on the inspected
reference facts, while the historical RePoE record is only a cross-check. PoB
is not adopted as authority for permanent-Mercenary mechanics; its model cannot
fill a missing live-game rule.

## Conflicts and gaps

The strongest structured source is pinned to 3.29.0, not 3.29.1. The current
community display is unpinned. The presence of high-level rows is not evidence
that those levels are ordinary or supported. The sources do not establish a
complete permanent-Mercenary link-state contract.

Most importantly, AUD-003 does not establish Golden Glory arithmetic,
direct-effect stacking/order, or all condition activation. No source establishes
the game rounding point for a fractional Maximum-Life component or a scaled
effect. The audit therefore preserves each input and gate separately rather
than treating an unknown contribution as zero.

PoB and RePoE licence terms for software do not grant a verified right to
redistribute copied Grinding Gear Games game data. The committed artifact has
only minimal identifiers, component mappings, anchor facts, source locators,
and retention notes. This is a risk record, not legal advice.

## Analysis

The evidence supports a reference representation with three distinct parts:
normal-level flat-added-Fire data, a source-Maximum-Life term, and duration
quality. The first two can be described separately, but the final number after
Link-effect scaling cannot be combined or rounded safely.

Future UX may show "level-based flat grant" and "5% of Luminary Maximum Life"
as provenance-bearing component descriptions. It must show an unavailable or
evidence-needed result, not `0`, whenever the link state, target eligibility,
source Maximum Life, dependency status, or rounding evidence is missing.

## Conclusion

`AUD-004` is supported for a bounded Flame Link reference contract, normal
level `1-20` review, component identity, standard-quality duration, and its
no-DPS presentation rule. It does not authorize the definitive scaled added
Fire Damage result. That result remains universally blocked by `AUD-003-C12`,
`AUD-004-C09`, and `AUD-004-C10`. `AUD-003-C08` additionally withholds only
the Powerful Bond contribution when that selected source is in scope.

## Non-conclusions

This audit does not establish live 3.29.1 numerical continuity, exceptional
level validity, active-link conditions, source ownership, automatic target
mapping, Golden Glory arithmetic, direct-effect stacking, rounding, damage
after penetration, DPS, or a combined score. It does not convert a PoB result
or an observed build value into a general game rule.

### Established rules

- Use the source Maximum Life of the link source, not current Life, reserved
  Life, or an invented Mercenary baseline.
- Keep normal level rows, exceptional-level review, the flat component, the
  Maximum-Life component, quality duration, and AUD-003 effect evidence
  separate until a supported formula joins them.
- At quality `20`, standard quality may be displayed as `+1,500 ms` base
  duration; no granted-damage change is inferred.
- Any eventual result is named "Added Fire Damage granted to linked Mercenary,"
  never DPS or total damage.

### Manually required or unsupported behavior

Manual review is required for exceptional levels, link/target state, and all
unknown conditions. A final scaled value is unavailable while `AUD-003-C12`,
`AUD-004-C09`, or `AUD-004-C10` is below its stated minimum. If Powerful Bond
is selected, `AUD-003-C08` withholds only that conditional contribution. Do not
use PoB rounding or modifier order as a substitute.

The fixture requires level `1` and `20` recognition; level `21` review; `0` and

and a consumer migration that recomputes or withdraws any dependent output.
