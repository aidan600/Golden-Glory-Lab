# AUD-005 - Enmity's Embrace calculation contract

## Status

supported

Contract version: `1.0.0`.

This audit supports a narrowly labelled, manual-input calculation of Enmity's
own Fire Penetration contribution. It does not authorize a permanent-Mercenary
sheet-derived path, total penetration, enemy effective resistance, damage, or
DPS.

## Question

What item facts and arithmetic can be retained for Enmity's Embrace, and what
must be manually supplied or withheld while the permanent-Mercenary sheet
semantics and item behavior remain incomplete?

## Product impact

The contract permits a future manual-only "Enmity's own Fire Penetration"
panel when the user attests final comparable inputs in one stated equipped and
conditional context. It also defines target/gap reporting for an explicit
Enmity-only objective. It does not permit a sheet-plus-equipment derivation,
an ownership inference, an improvement recommendation, or an aggregate combat
calculation.

## Scope

Included are a pinned supporting PoB 3.29 data snapshot, an unpinned current
PoEDB cross-check, the 3.29.1 target freeze, the AUD-002 manual-input contract,
and the first-release product-direction boundary.

Excluded are a claim that the item was unchanged in live 3.29.1, current
availability or acquisition, an item-set ownership inference, raw item tables,
how the ring's resistance penalty is ordered or rounded, aggregation with other
penetration, enemy resistance, damage, DPS, or production implementation.

## Source plan

The audit inspected the target patch notes, the already registered PoB dev
snapshot's unique-ring, stat-description, parser, and calculation references,
and the current PoEDB item page. It then binds only the supported item wording
and isolated arithmetic to the exact AUD-002 claim contract. No source code was
executed and no raw item table, page copy, image, asset, or player build was
retained.

## Evidence

The PoB dev snapshot contains Enmity's Embrace as a Vermillion Ring with a
required level of `80`, the relevant reviewed natural ranges, a `65-75%`
reduced Fire Resistance modifier, and a dynamic modifier represented as
`overcapped_fire_resistance_gain_as_fire_penetration`. Reproducible locators are
`src/Data/Uniques/ring.lua`, generated stat-description record `2919`,
`src/Modules/ModParser.lua`, `CalcDefence.lua`, and `CalcOffence.lua`; the item is present in PoB's 3.29 export commit
`3a73a54bd3a9c92c4d3264c5d697a51b6c9063bc`. Its associated description and
calculation model cap the ring's own contribution at `200`.

The reviewed short dynamic modifier wording is: "Damage Penetrates Fire
Resistance equal to your Overcapped Fire Resistance, up to a maximum of 200%."
It is retained as attributed audit evidence only, not as a runtime tooltip
payload. PoEDB currently corroborates the item wording and ranges, but neither
source is a version-pinned official 3.29.1 item record.

| Reviewed field | Minimal retained reference |
| --- | --- |
| Vermillion Ring base implicit Maximum Life | `5-7%` |
| Strength | `30-50` |
| Increased Fire Damage | `10-20%` |
| Reduced Fire Resistance magnitude | `65-75%` |
| Fire self-damage when using a Skill | `300-500` |
| Enmity modifier cap | `200%` item-specific Fire Penetration |

For final comparable inputs in one state, the item's displayed concept supports
the following narrow, isolated arithmetic:

```text
O = max(0, U - M)
P_enmity = min(200, O)
```

`U` is final Uncapped Fire Resistance *with Enmity equipped* and all relevant
effects in the recorded state. `M` is final Maximum Fire Resistance in that
same state. `P_enmity` is the ring's own Fire Penetration contribution only.
The `200` is an item-specific cap, not evidence of a global Fire Penetration
cap. The formulas deliberately do not derive `U` from a baseline, components,
or the ring's reduction penalty.

For an explicit Enmity-only target `T` from `0` through `200`, supported
product reporting is:

```text
gap = max(0, T - P_enmity)
surplus = max(0, P_enmity - T)
capHeadroom = max(0, 200 - P_enmity)
inputBeyondCap = max(0, O - 200)
```

A target below `0` is invalid. A target above `200` is
`unreachable-by-Enmity`, not an ordinary gap. These are product comparisons
around the isolated item contribution; they make no claim about a global cap,
other sources, enemies, or damage.

The evidence does not establish the ordering, rounding, below-zero handling,
or display precision of the ring's `reduced Fire Resistance` penalty. It also
does not resolve a current availability conflict: the reviewed PoB snapshot and
PoEDB list different acquisition labels: the reviewed PoB snapshot names
"Incarnation of Fear / Moment of Trauma," while PoEDB names "Incarnation of
Fear / The Market." The audit preserves the disagreement rather than selecting
either source as current availability.

## Conflicts and gaps

The item facts are supported by a PoB development snapshot and an unpinned
community page, not an official 3.29.1 item record. The two sources disagree
on acquisition wording. The audit makes no conclusion about availability,
tradeability, legacy status, owner mapping, or recommendation.

The largest load-bearing gap is not the algebra: it is the meaning and
comparability of a permanent-Mercenary sheet value. AUD-002 leaves labels,
inclusion, conditions, and context unknown. Deriving `U` by adding an item
penalty or other components could double-count, omit a state, or adopt PoB
rounding without evidence.

PoB's software licence does not establish redistribution rights for GGG item
data, and the community-page terms do not resolve the game's underlying data
rights. Only minimal factual fields, source locators, and synthetic fixture
inputs are committed. This is a risk record, not legal advice.

## Analysis

The contract separates two paths. A user may manually supply final `U` and `M`
for the same state only after explicitly selecting
`enmity.equipped-state=equipped` and confirming that `U` already includes
Enmity. For integral manually attested values, the isolated arithmetic has supported
product expectations. If a supplied value has fractional precision, the
product preserves it but reports `rounding-evidence-required` rather than
inventing a live-game rounding rule.

The sheet-derived path is deliberately narrower than the manual path. It cannot
infer a baseline or add the ring's reduction penalty because AUD-002 does not
establish the needed fields or inclusion state. It must retain the user input,
explain the affected claims, and avoid disabling unrelated review.

## Conclusion

`AUD-005` supports a manual-only, isolated Enmity contribution and explicit
target/gap reporting for user-attested comparable integral inputs. It does not
support a sheet-derived calculation, aggregate penetration, or damage model.
The derived path remains blocked by `AUD-002-C03`, `AUD-002-C04`,
`AUD-002-C05`, `AUD-005-C05`, and `AUD-005-C07`.

## Non-conclusions

This audit does not confirm the item is unchanged in live 3.29.1, resolve its
availability, establish an owner, infer a Mercenary baseline, default Maximum
Fire Resistance to `75`, recalculate the ring penalty, combine other
penetration, estimate enemy resistance, calculate damage/DPS, or create a
combined score. It does not turn a raw imported item or a personal sheet into
general reference evidence.

## Implementation contract

Version: `1.0.0`.

### Intended downstream consumer

A future Enmity reference/review panel, the AUD-002 manual-input form, an
isolated manual-only contribution reporter, and target/gap reporting consume
this contract. A production calculator must inspect exact dependency claims,
not this audit's headline status.

### Upstream dependencies and gating

`AUD-005` consumes `AUD-002` contract `1.0.0` exactly as follows:

| Upstream claim | Required use | Minimum status | Behavior when provisional, unknown, superseded, or version-mismatched |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `AUD-002-C02` | Permanent-Mercenary input scope | supported | Do not offer a permanent-Mercenary-specific sheet adapter. |
| `AUD-002-C03` | Field semantics, units, cap/uncapped meaning, and Maximum Fire Resistance identity | supported for a derived path | Withhold derived value and preserve manual final values. |
| `AUD-002-C04` | Sheet/equipment inclusion | supported for any component derivation | Never add a ring penalty or mapped component to a sheet value; require final manual values. |
| `AUD-002-C05` | Comparable context | supported for a derived path | Withhold derived result; require one manually stated shared context for `U` and `M`. |
| `AUD-002-C06` | Manual-only final comparable values | supported | Do not calculate even the manual path until this safety contract is supported. |

The manual path consumes `AUD-002-C06` and never upgrades the meaning of the
entered values. The derived path additionally requires `C03-C05`. If a status
or contract version changes, dependent output must be recomputed or withdrawn;
it must not silently retain a prior result.

### Established rules

- `enmity.equipped-state` must be explicitly `equipped` before manual
  `P_enmity` is reported.
- `U` is final with Enmity already equipped; never apply the ring's resistance
  penalty a second time.
- `U` and `M` must describe the same equipped and conditional state.
- For manually attested integral inputs, use only the isolated formula from
  `AUD-005-C03` and `AUD-005-C04` and label its output as Enmity's own Fire
  Penetration contribution.
- Preserve arbitrary observed values and original material; natural ranges are
  review metadata, not validation bounds.
- Keep Enmity-only target reporting separate from enemy resistance, other
  penetration, damage, DPS, and recommendations.

### Manually required or unsupported behavior

Manual final inputs, context, and explicit `enmity.equipped-state=equipped` are
required. Fractional values remain reviewable but do not receive an invented
rounded game result. Sheet-plus-equipment derivation, item ownership inference,
availability, total penetration, enemy resistance, and damage are unsupported.

Report `manual-final-input`, `not-applicable`, `derived-unavailable`, `missing`,
`unknown`, `rounding-evidence-required`, `recognized`, `outside-reviewed-natural-range`,
`unreviewed-availability`, `unreachable-by-Enmity`, `provisional`,
`superseded`, and `version-mismatched` independently. Never turn a missing or
unknown input into zero.

### Acceptance examples

The fixture requires manual integral cases `(75,75)->0`, `(76,75)->1`,
`(275,75)->200`, and `(300,75)->200` with `inputBeyondCap=25`; zero-overcap
handling for `U=0` and `U=-60` without a display claim; target-state handling;
a sheet-derived inclusion-state gate; explicit equipped/not-equipped/unknown
Enmity attestation states; a fractional manual input gate; and an out-of-range
raw observed roll preserved without clamping.

review, and a consumer migration that recomputes or withdraws affected output.
